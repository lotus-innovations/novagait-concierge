# General FAQ

All content in this document is synthetic demo data for the Novagait Physical Therapy demonstration clinic.

## Does physical therapy hurt?

Some soreness during and after sessions is normal, especially early in a plan of care — similar to post-workout soreness. Your therapist works within your tolerance and adjusts intensity every visit. Sharp or lasting pain is never the goal; report it and the plan will be modified.

## How many visits will I need?

Most plans of care run 6 to 12 visits over 4 to 8 weeks, but it varies with the condition, your goals, and how consistently the home program gets done. Your therapist gives you an expected visit count at the initial evaluation and updates it as you progress.

## Can I go to the gym while in PT?

Usually yes, with guidance. Bring your current routine to the initial evaluation and your therapist will mark what to keep, modify, or pause.

## Do you treat car accident (personal injury) cases?

We treat patients recovering from motor vehicle accidents. Personal injury protection (PIP) and attorney-lien billing arrangements are handled case by case by the billing team; bring your claim number and adjuster contact to the first visit.

## Can I choose Novagait if my doctor referred me elsewhere?

Yes. Patients have the legal right to choose their physical therapy provider regardless of where a referral was directed.

## What is the anti-gravity treadmill?

The Crestline Commons clinic has a body-weight-supporting treadmill that lets you walk or run at a reduced percentage of your body weight. It is used for early post-surgical gait work and return-to-running progressions.

## Do you offer massage?

Manual therapy (hands-on soft tissue and joint work) is part of treatment when clinically appropriate, but we do not offer standalone massage appointments.
