# Services & Treatment Programs

All content in this document is synthetic demo data for the Novagait Physical Therapy demonstration clinic.

## Core services

Novagait Physical Therapy offers outpatient orthopedic and neurological rehabilitation:

- **Initial evaluation** (60 minutes): a licensed physical therapist assesses movement, strength, pain patterns, and history, then builds an individualized plan of care.
- **Orthopedic rehabilitation**: back and neck pain, shoulder injuries, hip and knee conditions, foot and ankle injuries, and repetitive-strain conditions.
- **Post-surgical rehabilitation**: structured recovery programs after joint replacement (hip, knee, shoulder), ACL reconstruction, rotator cuff repair, and spinal surgery. We coordinate with your surgeon's protocol.
- **Sports rehabilitation and return-to-play**: sport-specific strengthening, agility progressions, and objective return-to-play testing.
- **Balance and fall-prevention (vestibular) therapy**: for dizziness, vertigo, and fall risk, including BPPV repositioning.
- **Gait analysis and training**: video-assisted walking and running analysis with corrective exercise programs. This is our signature program, reflected in the clinic name.
- **Dry needling**: offered as an adjunct by certified providers, with your consent, when clinically appropriate.

## Typical visit structure

Follow-up visits are 45 minutes: hands-on treatment, guided exercise, and home-program updates. Most plans of care run 6 to 12 visits over 4 to 8 weeks, adjusted to your progress.

## What we do not offer

We do not provide medical diagnosis, imaging (X-ray/MRI), medication prescriptions, injections, chiropractic adjustments, or massage-only appointments. For those needs we will refer you back to your physician.
