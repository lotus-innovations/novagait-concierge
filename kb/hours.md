# Hours & Scheduling Policies

All content in this document is synthetic demo data for the Novagait Physical Therapy demonstration clinic.

## Clinic hours

- **Crestline Commons clinic**: Monday to Thursday 7:00am - 7:00pm, Friday 7:00am - 5:00pm, Saturday 8:00am - 12:00pm, closed Sunday.
- **Harborview Plaza clinic**: Monday to Friday 8:00am - 6:00pm, closed weekends.
- **Telehealth**: appointments available Monday to Friday 7:00am - 6:00pm.

## Holidays

Both clinics are closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Holiday-week hours may be reduced; the front desk confirms when booking.

## Scheduling

New patients are typically seen within 2 business days. You can book through this assistant, by phone, or at the front desk. Standing recurring slots (same day/time each week) can be arranged with the front desk for the duration of a plan of care.

## Running late

If you expect to be more than 15 minutes late, call the clinic; we may need to shorten or reschedule the visit so other patients are not delayed.
