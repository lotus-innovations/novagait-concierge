# New Patient Onboarding

All content in this document is synthetic demo data for the Novagait Physical Therapy demonstration clinic.

## Before your first visit

1. Complete the intake forms sent by email after booking (health history, consent, insurance details). Online completion takes about 10 minutes; arriving 15 minutes early to do them on paper also works.
2. Send or bring any relevant documents: physician referral (if you have one), surgical protocol, imaging reports. We do not need the images themselves, just the written report.
3. We run a complimentary insurance benefits check and send you a written cost estimate before the first appointment.

## What to bring

- Photo ID and insurance card
- Referral or surgical protocol if applicable
- A list of current medications
- Payment method for any copay

## What to wear

Comfortable clothing you can move in. For lower-body concerns bring or wear shorts; for shoulder concerns a tank top or loose shirt helps. Athletic shoes are best for gait analysis visits.

## Your first appointment

The initial evaluation lasts 60 minutes: history and goal-setting, movement and strength assessment, hands-on examination, a first round of treatment, and a home exercise starter program. You leave with a written plan of care, expected visit frequency, and your exercise program in the patient portal app.

## Minors

Patients under 18 need a parent or guardian present at the first visit and a signed consent form for subsequent unaccompanied visits.
