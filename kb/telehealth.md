# Telehealth Policy

All content in this document is synthetic demo data for the Novagait Physical Therapy demonstration clinic.

## What can be done over telehealth

Telehealth works well for: follow-up visits within an existing plan of care, home exercise program reviews and progressions, chronic pain self-management coaching, post-surgical check-ins between hands-on visits, and vestibular exercise supervision. Telehealth visits are 30 minutes.

## What requires an in-person visit

Initial evaluations, any visit requiring hands-on treatment (manual therapy, dry needling), formal gait-analysis lab sessions, and return-to-play testing must be in person. If a telehealth visit reveals something that needs hands-on assessment, your therapist will convert the next visit to in-person.

## Technology requirements

A smartphone, tablet, or computer with a camera, microphone, and stable internet connection. A private space where you can move safely — enough room to stand and take a few steps. The secure video link arrives by email and text 30 minutes before the visit; no app installation is required.

## Insurance coverage for telehealth

Most demo plans (Meridian Health PPO, Cascadia Blue) cover telehealth physical therapy at the same rate as in-person visits. Original Medicare coverage of telehealth PT is confirmed during your benefits check. Workers' compensation cases require insurer pre-approval for telehealth.

## State licensing

Telehealth visits require you to be physically located in a state where your therapist is licensed at the time of the visit. Confirm with the front desk if you plan to attend a telehealth visit while traveling.
