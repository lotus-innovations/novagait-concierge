# Cancellation & No-Show Policy

All content in this document is synthetic demo data for the Novagait Physical Therapy demonstration clinic.

## Cancellation window

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a visit. Cancel by phone, through the patient portal, or through this assistant's human handoff (cancellations are confirmed by a person, not completed automatically).

## Late cancellation and no-show fees

- Cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice: $50 fee
- No-show (no notice): $75 fee

Fees are charged to the card on file and are not billable to insurance. The first late cancellation on any account is automatically waived as a courtesy.

## Fee waivers

Fees are waived for sudden illness, family emergencies, and unsafe travel conditions (for example, snow or ice advisories). Tell the front desk what happened; we err on the side of waiving.

## Repeated missed visits

After three no-shows in a plan of care we pause scheduling and ask you to speak with your therapist about whether the plan still fits your life. Consistency drives outcomes in physical therapy, and we would rather adjust the plan than charge fees.

## Waitlist

If you cancel, your slot is offered to waitlisted patients. If you want earlier appointments, ask to join the waitlist and we will text you when a slot opens.
