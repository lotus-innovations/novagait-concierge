# Insurance FAQ

All content in this document is synthetic demo data for the Novagait Physical Therapy demonstration clinic.

## Which insurance plans do you accept?

We are in-network with the following synthetic demo plans: Meridian Health PPO, Cascadia Blue HMO and PPO, TrailPoint Medicare Advantage, original Medicare Part B, and Lantern Workers' Compensation. We are out-of-network with all other plans but can provide a superbill for self-submission.

## Do I need a referral or prescription?

Our state allows direct access to physical therapy, so you can start without a physician referral for up to 30 days or 10 visits, whichever comes first. Some insurance plans (notably TrailPoint Medicare Advantage and all workers' compensation cases) still require a physician referral for coverage. Our front desk verifies this during benefits check.

## What will I owe per visit?

It depends on your plan's deductible, copay, and coinsurance. Before your first visit we run a complimentary benefits verification and give you a written cost estimate. Typical in-network copays for demo plans range from $20 to $45 per visit.

## Do you handle prior authorization?

Yes. If your plan requires prior authorization (common for HMO plans and visits beyond the initial approval), our billing team submits it and tracks approvals. Treatment never starts on an unauthorized visit without your written consent to self-pay.

## What about Medicare?

We accept original Medicare Part B assignment. Medicare covers medically necessary physical therapy; the annual therapy threshold tracking and required documentation are handled by our billing team.

## Billing questions

Billing statements come from Novagait Physical Therapy Billing, and our billing line is answered Monday through Friday, 9am to 4pm. Bring billing questions to the front desk or request a callback through this assistant's human handoff.
