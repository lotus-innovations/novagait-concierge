# Pricing & Self-Pay Rates

All content in this document is synthetic demo data for the Novagait Physical Therapy demonstration clinic. All prices are fictional demo values.

## Self-pay rates

For patients without insurance or choosing not to use it:

- Initial evaluation (60 min): $165
- Follow-up visit (45 min): $120
- Telehealth follow-up (30 min): $85
- Gait analysis lab session with written report (75 min): $210
- Dry needling add-on: $40

Self-pay visits are due at time of service. A 10-visit follow-up package is available for $1,080 (10% savings), valid for 6 months.

## Good faith estimates

Under the No Surprises Act, self-pay patients receive a written good faith estimate of expected charges before starting care.

## Insurance pricing

If we are in-network with your plan, your cost is set by your plan's copay, deductible, and coinsurance, not by the rates above. See the Insurance FAQ document; we verify benefits and give a written estimate before your first visit.

## Payment methods

We accept credit and debit cards, HSA/FSA cards, checks, and cash. Payment plans can be arranged with the billing team for balances over $200.

## Financial hardship

A sliding-scale program is available for patients experiencing financial hardship; ask the front desk for an application. Applications are reviewed within one week and are confidential.
