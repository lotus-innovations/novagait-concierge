# Home Exercise Program & Patient Portal

All content in this document is synthetic demo data for the Novagait Physical Therapy demonstration clinic.

## Your home program

Every plan of care includes a home exercise program. Your therapist assigns exercises with photos, videos, and dose (sets, reps, frequency) in the patient portal app, and updates them as you progress. Doing the home program consistently is the single biggest predictor of a good outcome.

## The patient portal

The portal app (iOS, Android, and web) shows your exercise program, lets you log completed sessions and pain levels, message the front desk, view upcoming appointments, and pay balances. An invitation email arrives after your first visit.

## Equipment

Most home programs need little or no equipment. When something is required (resistance bands, a foam roller), we provide starter bands at the clinic at no charge and suggest inexpensive retail options for anything else.

## Trouble with an exercise

If an exercise causes sharp pain, stop it and message the clinic through the portal; your therapist will adjust the program, usually the same business day. Do not push through sharp pain between visits.

## After discharge

At discharge you keep portal access and a maintenance program. Alumni patients can book a single tune-up visit at any time without restarting a full plan of care.
