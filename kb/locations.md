# Locations & Parking

All content in this document is synthetic demo data for the Novagait Physical Therapy demonstration clinic.

## Crestline Commons clinic

1840 Crestline Commons Way, Suite 210 (second floor, elevator access). Our largest clinic, with the full gait-analysis lab, anti-gravity treadmill, and turf strip for sports rehab. Free two-hour parking in the Commons garage; enter on Aldergrove Street and take a ticket for validation at our front desk.

## Harborview Plaza clinic

422 Harborview Plaza, Suite 105 (ground floor, step-free entrance). A smaller neighborhood clinic focused on orthopedic and post-surgical care. Street parking plus a small surface lot behind the building; two accessible spaces directly outside the entrance.

## Telehealth

Telehealth visits are conducted over our secure video platform; a link arrives by email and text 30 minutes before the visit. See the Telehealth Policy document for eligibility.

## Accessibility

Both clinics are wheelchair accessible, have accessible restrooms, and offer accessible parking. If you need any accommodation (interpreter services, mobility assistance from the parking area), tell us when booking and we will arrange it at no cost.

## Public transit

Crestline Commons is served by the 12 and 47 bus lines (Aldergrove & 18th stop). Harborview Plaza is two blocks from the Harborview light-rail station.
